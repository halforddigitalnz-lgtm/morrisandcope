Flying the business

You fly it. We watch the instruments.

Your dashboard

Just as a pilot needs a clear dashboard to fly a plane, a business owner needs reliable financial data to navigate their company. Without it, you're flying blind. Morris & Cope provides that essential dashboard of financial information, helping you steer your business proactively through economic storms and toward new opportunities.

Your expert co-pilot

Outsourcing your bookkeeping to us is like having an experienced co-pilot by your side. We'll take the hassle and grind out of the day-to-day accounts administration, freeing up your valuable time to focus on what you do best: growing your business. What takes you hours, we can do in a fraction of the time.

Navigate with confidence

We provide practical management information in a language you can understand. You'll gain the confidence to analyse financial reports and make informed strategic decisions, avoiding pitfalls before they become major problems — so you can worry less and enjoy the work-life balance you've always wanted.

Meet the team

The person who will actually do your books

David Armstrong

Director

David brings extensive experience in business operations and management, with a strong focus on systems implementation and operational efficiency. He is committed to helping clients experience these efficiencies, allowing them to achieve more accurate and timely financial management and better business outcomes.

His background spans operations management, production planning, pricing, estimating, and bookkeeping, giving him a broad and practical understanding of how businesses run. David's passion for finance began early in his career, when he saw first-hand that financial health underpins every good business decision. This insight continues to shape his approach today: investing in streamlined processes now leads to greater clarity, control, and long-term benefits for every client.
