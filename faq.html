Frequently asked questions

The questions business owners ask us most often. If yours isn't here, call and ask — the first conversation is free.

A bookkeeper can ensure that your financial records are accurate, up-to-date, and organised, and leave you to work on growing your business, looking after your customers, and focusing on the parts of your work you enjoy most.

They also give you peace of mind that your GST, payroll, and compliance obligations are being taken care of, reducing stress and the risk of costly mistakes.

It really depends on your business size, transaction volume, and reporting needs. As a rule of thumb, we recommend weekly reconciliations to keep everything accurate and up to date.

On top of that, we provide monthly reports as standard, giving you a clear picture of your business performance and helping you set aside the right amount for tax.

Bookkeepers record daily financial transactions, such as sales, purchases, and payments. They can help with invoicing to clients and managing your accounts payable. They can also process payroll and file PAYE, and prepare and file GST returns.

Accountants use the data provided by the bookkeeper to analyse the business's financial performance, prepare annual financial statements, and file annual income tax returns.

We'll have an initial phone conversation to get broad details about your business and bookkeeping needs. Then we'll meet to talk in more detail about your existing systems and the services that we provide so that you can select the ones you want.

From that, we'll send you a quote. After you accept the quote, you'll provide the necessary financial documents (we'll tell you what we need) and Xero access.

After the initial onboarding, you will need to supply bills and receipts so that we can record your expenses and accounts payable. The more organised the input, the smoother and cheaper the bookkeeping, so we encourage you — and provide set up and training — to do this digitally.

We provide monthly reporting as standard to all our clients, with training, where necessary, to help you understand those reports. A monthly commentary on your business performance is an optional extra.

We use Xero for our clients and can take care of setup and transitioning from other software or from manual systems. We can also provide training if you would like to use Xero yourself.
